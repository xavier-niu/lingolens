Night Trains of Europe

Night trains are coming back across Europe. You board in one city after dinner, fall asleep to the sound of the wheels, and wake up in another country in time for breakfast.

The cabins are small but clever: the seats fold into beds, there is a tiny sink in the corner, and a window that shows dark fields and quiet stations going by.
